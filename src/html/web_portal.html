Learn (adaptive profile tuning after each throw) Learn adjusts the selected profile's fine and coarse max speed and fine Kp in RAM, bounded to 50% to 140% of the starting values. Save the profile from the Profiles page to keep the result.
Lag Compensation Drives the motors off reading + rate x lag so the tubes slow before the scale catches up. The final stop is still on the real reading. Learn Powder measures the lag and turns this on.
Scale Lag (s)
Auto Lag Re-measures the lag on every throw (what landed after the motors stopped, divided by how fast the reading was climbing) and tracks it slowly. Live values are on the trickler page.
